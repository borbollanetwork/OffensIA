# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in OffensIA a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith; disagree technically, not personally.
- Use OffensIA and discuss its use only in the context of authorized,
  ethical security work.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Encouraging or facilitating unauthorized intrusion or illegal activity.
- Sharing another person's private information without consent.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this code, and may ban repeat or severe offenders. Report concerns privately to the
maintainers.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
